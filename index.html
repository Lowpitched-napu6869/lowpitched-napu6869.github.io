marvelrivals-galacta is a powerful yet simple mod loader and skin swapper for Marvel Rivals. It lets you change how your characters look in the game — from comic-inspired suits to completely original designs — all with a few clicks.

🛠️ How Does It Work?

Think of it like a magic projector. The game normally shows you a default skin for each hero. marvelrivals-galacta "projects" your chosen custom skin over the default one while you play. When you close the game, the projection disappears — your original game files stay 100% untouched.

  • Runtime Modding: Mods mount at runtime (when you start the game), so nothing is permanently written to disk.
  • No Ban Risk: Because game files aren't modified, it's designed to be safe and undetectable.
